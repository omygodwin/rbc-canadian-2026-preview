Relative to the tour, off the tee was the one distinctively-rewarded skill in 2025 — putting was just its usual noisy self.

What mattered MORE here than tour-wide

Raw correlations mislead: putting tracks single-event finish at nearly every stop (it's noisy, and making putts is simply how you score), so a big putting r tells you nothing course-specific. What matters is how much more (or less) a category predicted finish here than it does across all 34 venues. Accuracy vs Bombing — which is the bigger lever?

The right way to answer it: what does missing the fairway cost (rough vs fairway scoring penalty), and what does extra distance buy (do longer drivers actually score better here)? Both ranked against the 37-venue field.

Read: missing the fairway costs +0.37 strokes/hole here — #9 of 34 venues (76th percentile), a steep penalty. Extra distance is worth a 60th-percentile edge (#15/36). Both off-the-tee skills are above-average levers, but the fairway-miss penalty edges it — accuracy is the slightly bigger driver. Translation: get it in the short grass first; your length then helps, it doesn't bail you out.
