The Setup — A One-Year Book

The RBC Canadian Open rotates venues, and TPC Toronto at Osprey Valley (North Course) debuted as host in 2025 — so the PGA Tour has exactly one year of shot-tracking history here. That's the entire course book, and this preview leans on it plus 2026 season form. Because the venue is new to the rotation (not a renovation of an old host), there's no layout drift to reconcile — the 2025 data reflects today's course one-for-one. Treat every venue read as a small sample.

2025 champion: Ryan Fox won at −18, edging Sam Burns (also −18) — a low-scoring, gettable week. The North Course gave up red numbers: the field averaged -0.07 to par per hole across the 4 rounds.
